\begin{englishabstract}%
  {Automated Semantic Reconstruction of Domestic Environments for Robot Simulation}%
  {neural reconstruction, robot simulation, 3D geometry, DM-NeRF}

Neural scene reconstruction can reduce modeling effort but does not directly provide the structure required by robotic simulators. This dissertation evaluates the transformation of an instance-decomposed DM-NeRF reconstruction into an explicit scene for Gazebo Classic. The study uses the DM-SR \textit{study} scene and analyzes appearance, geometry, object organization, and simulator operation separately. On 100 test views, the reconstruction achieved 41.1100 dB PSNR, 0.9875 SSIM, and 0.0260 LPIPS. In the geometric evaluation, the mesh F-score ranged from 0.0480 at 1 cm to 0.7750 at 5 cm; on the same spatial support, COLMAP achieved 0.0285 and 0.3721. Post-processing produced 13 independent models, treated the support surface geometrically, and generated an executable Gazebo scene. The results demonstrate the feasibility of this conversion for the evaluated scene and protocol and show the influence of distance tolerance on geometric correspondence.
